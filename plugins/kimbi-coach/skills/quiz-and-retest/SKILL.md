# Quiz and retest

Test retrieval and reasoning, not recognition alone.

## Quiz setup

Establish the source scope, question count or time limit, format, and desired strictness. If unspecified, use five mixed questions delivered one at a time, with no answer reveal before an attempt.

Mix appropriate question types:

- explain or distinguish concepts;
- classify accounts or transactions;
- prepare an entry or calculation;
- find an error;
- predict financial-statement effects;
- apply a rule to a changed scenario.

Do not let wording or answer patterns reveal the answer. For multiple choice, use distractors derived from realistic misconceptions.

## Scoring

After each response, record correctness, confidence if provided, error type, and the smallest missing idea. Do not count a lucky choice as secure mastery when the explanation is wrong. At the end, report:

- score and topic breakdown;
- secure, fragile, and missing knowledge;
- high-confidence errors;
- the two highest-value review actions.

Retest missed or fragile ideas with new surface details and the same underlying reasoning. Space the suggested retest interval according to performance: soon for missing knowledge, later for correct and well-explained answers. Follow the integrity and tone principles in [coach principles](../../references/coach-principles.md).
