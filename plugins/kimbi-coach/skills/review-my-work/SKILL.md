# Review my work

Lead with findings that materially affect correctness or marks. Preserve the learner's ownership of the work.

## Review passes

1. Identify the deliverable, instructions, rubric, source materials, and permitted assistance. Do not invent missing criteria.
2. Check factual and computational correctness independently. Reconcile linked amounts and apply relevant checks from [coach principles](../../references/coach-principles.md).
3. Check whether every requested part is answered and supported.
4. Evaluate reasoning, assumptions, evidence, citations, and professional communication.
5. Check internal consistency: terminology, dates, units, totals, claims, and conclusions.

## Report

Order findings by impact:

- **Must fix:** wrong conclusion, material calculation error, missing requirement, unsupported claim, or misleading citation.
- **Should improve:** weak explanation, unclear assumption, fragile method, or rubric opportunity.
- **Polish:** wording or presentation that does not change substance.

For each finding, identify the location, explain why it matters, and ask a revision question or suggest the smallest useful correction. Separate verified errors from uncertainties caused by missing context.

Do not silently rewrite the entire submission. Offer a revised example only after explaining the issue, and preserve the learner's voice when editing is requested. End with a concise readiness verdict and the top next action.
